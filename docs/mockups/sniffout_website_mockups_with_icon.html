About this walk

Richmond Park's famous deer make this one of the most spectacular walks in London. Keep dogs on leads throughout - a legal requirement. The Pen Ponds are a favourite stop, and on a clear day the views stretch all the way to St Paul's.

For your dog
On-lead throughout. Water at the Pen Ponds. Deer present year-round - give extra space October to November during the rut.
