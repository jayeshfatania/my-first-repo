What you are deciding

  • Header pill: sienna gives the CTA significantly more visual pop against the warm off-white header. Low risk, high impact.
  • Difficulty badges: colour-coding adds genuine scannability. Sienna for Moderate is warm, not alarming. This is the clearest functional win.
  • Walk cards: sienna on "Sniffout Pick" badge distinguishes it from the brand green content chips. The two colours now have distinct roles -- green for dog-specific info, sienna for editorial curation.
  • Homepage hero CTA: Option A (keep white) is the recommended default. Option B available if you want more personality once sienna is established elsewhere.
