Box Hill's north face offers one of the most rewarding ridge walks in Surrey. The route climbs steadily through mixed woodland before breaking out onto an open chalk ridge with views stretching south across the Weald and north toward the Thames corridor.

It is a walk that rewards patience. The first kilometre asks for effort, following a well-worn chalk track that can be slippery after rain. But once the tree line opens up, the gradient eases and the path follows the ridge edge for a long, airy stretch that dogs and people tend to walk with equal enthusiasm.

When the ridge opens up and the whole Weald spreads out below you, it is hard not to stop and just look for a while.

The descent back through Juniper Bottom is shaded and quieter than the main trail, passing a small stream crossing that most dogs will wade into regardless of the season. The loop closes through farmland at the southern edge of the National Trust estate, returning to the main car park via the stepping stones crossing.
